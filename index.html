About Me

Hi, I’m MD Nahiduzzaman Dipu, a passionate developer, designer, and digital marketing specialist from Dhaka, Bangladesh. Currently, I am pursuing my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the Green University of Bangladesh. As a developer, I have strong expertise in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python—with a particular focus on Machine Learning, where Python plays a key role. On the creative side, I bring my ideas to life through Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and other design tools, delivering visually impactful and professional designs. Alongside development and design, I am also skilled in digital marketing. I help businesses grow by creating effective strategies in SEO, lead generation, and ad campaign management, ensuring measurable results and sustainable online presence. Beyond my professional skills, I’m a person who loves exploring the world and capturing it through my lens. My hobbies include traveling, fishing, gardening, and photography, with photography being one of my greatest passions. I thrive at the intersection of technology, creativity, and strategy, and I’m always eager to learn, create, and contribute.
